Unit detail

Change vs Aug is in percentage points. Flag marks units above the group rate.
ASLL is not in the Sep'26 sheet, so it is not included. Totals match the sheet's Overall row: 3,783 opening, 3,845 closing, 115 leavers. ABSL's sheet shows 3.25%. Applying the formula to 3 leavers on 110 opening and 107 closing gives 2.76%, which is used here. All other unit rates match the sheet. For FAL, AEL Mustard Oil and ARMCL-3 the Aug rate is taken from the August sheet, because the Sep sheet leaves it blank or shows a different value. Reported new-joiner counts in the Sep'26 remarks do not reconcile to the headcount movement, so they are not shown.
